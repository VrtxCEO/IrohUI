Add Eyro to your phone

Eyro runs as an app on your home screen, not in a browser tab. Install it, then pair it with your Eyro.

Android

  1. Open the browser menu ⋮
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen
  3. Open Eyro from your home screen

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  4. Open Eyro from your home screen
Installed, Eyro opens full screen and can notify you when an agent needs you. Notifications on iPhone only work once it's on your home screen.
